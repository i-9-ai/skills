## Approach

Record the chosen design and the material alternatives considered. Explain task value, resource cost, dependencies, and why the responsibility remains cohesive. Distinguish evidence from assumptions.

## Contract

Specify workflow boundaries, output shape, necessary resources, `LICENSE` and attribution, semantic tool capabilities, authority, secret handling, failures, stopping conditions, and rollback. Identify any companion handoffs.

## Evaluation and handoff

List observable acceptance criteria, representative cases, iteration budget, unresolved decisions, and the creator's next action. A coherent design is not proof that the implementation has passed evaluation.
