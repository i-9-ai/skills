## Approach

Record the chosen design and the material alternatives considered. Explain task value, resource and context cost, dependencies, and why the responsibility remains cohesive. Distinguish evidence from assumptions. When representation affects a material outcome, state how prose, table, Mermaid, code or schema, and on-demand references were compared, plus any optional optimization handoff.

## Contract

Specify workflow boundaries, output shape, necessary resources, `LICENSE` and attribution, semantic tool capabilities, authority, secret handling, failures, stopping conditions, and rollback. Preserve a domain dossier's source scope/date, conflicts, and specialist-confirmation gates as explicit design preconditions when it applies. Distinguish installed resource paths from the caller's output workspace and identify any companion dependencies and unavailable handoffs.

## Evaluation and handoff

List observable acceptance criteria, representative cases, iteration budget, unresolved decisions, and the caller's next action. A coherent design is not proof that the implementation has passed evaluation.
