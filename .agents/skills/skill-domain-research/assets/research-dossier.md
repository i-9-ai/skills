## Search log

Record queries or retrieval paths, source classes targeted, search date, refinements, and gaps. Public queries must be sanitized so they do not expose private process facts.

## Findings

### Supported external facts

State only conclusions supported within the recorded scope. Cite source IDs.

### Reported internal practice

State what the supplied process currently does, who or what reported it, and the observed scope/date. Do not restate it as a public rule.

### Inferences and qualifications

Explain the reasoning and what would change it. Keep inference visibly separate from source statements.

### Conflicts, unknowns, and contrary evidence

Preserve unresolved disagreement, missing authority, stale evidence, and failed search paths. Explain why each item matters to the future skill.

## Normative and specialist-confirmation gate

For each consequential normative finding, record the applicable jurisdiction,
date, controlling source status, and unresolved applicability or interpretation
questions. Collecting and synthesizing that evidence does not require a
professional approval gate.

For a case-specific professional determination or advice, additionally identify
the exact conclusion, the qualified specialist needed, and the decision that
must await that person's validation. Preserve the usable research findings and
their limitations while that separate determination remains pending.
