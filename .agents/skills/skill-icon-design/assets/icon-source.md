## Reuse and modifications

Reuse decision: adapt the 24-pixel Material glyph to this skill's responsibility. The palette holes use smooth cubic shorthand in place of explicit cubic segments; the receipt does not claim byte-identical path data. The local design adds a 64-pixel gradient frame, backing circle, accessible title, white glyph color and translation/scale; non-visible upstream canvas geometry is not retained. These modifications are by i-9-ai. `icon.png` is the bundled large rendering of the local SVG.
